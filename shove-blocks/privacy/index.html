Privacy Policy: Shove Blocks

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Shove Blocks ("the app") is developed by Cresponchilas ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location. You do not need an account to play.

Your game progress (scores, settings, power-ups and the current game) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver, measure, and personalize ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interaction data and diagnostic information.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or a region with similar laws, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
